Are petrol and toll fees included in the charter rate? +
Yes! All charter prices include clean private air-conditioned vehicle, petrol, parking, toll fees, and driver service for the specified duration.
Are attraction entrance tickets included? +
Charter rates cover transportation only. Temple entrance tickets, activity fees (e.g., swing, water sports), and meals are paid directly by guests.
Can we customize our own itinerary? +
Absolutely! All tours are 100% private and flexible. You can adjust stopping points or timing anytime during the day.
